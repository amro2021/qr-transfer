Show the QR stream on your phone so the people around you can receive the quiz from you. It stays locked for them too. Turn your screen brightness up.

How to run it
  1. Before class: add one file per group, then encrypt. Print the code slips or keep the codes.
  2. At the start of class: play the stream fullscreen on the projector. Students receive it from their seats. Those who finish can share it with their neighbours.
  3. When everyone shows Quiz received: hand out the slips, or show a code on screen. Everyone opens the quiz at the same moment.
